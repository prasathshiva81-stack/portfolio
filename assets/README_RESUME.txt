RESUME SETUP INSTRUCTIONS
=========================

To enable the "Download CV" button on your portfolio:

1. Add your resume file to this assets folder
2. Name it: resume.pdf
3. The download button will automatically work

Alternative: Use Google Drive or Dropbox
----------------------------------------
If you prefer to host your resume online:

1. Upload your resume to Google Drive or Dropbox
2. Get the shareable link
3. In index.html, replace:
   href="assets/resume.pdf"
   with your Google Drive/Dropbox direct download link

Google Drive direct download format:
https://drive.google.com/uc?export=download&id=YOUR_FILE_ID
